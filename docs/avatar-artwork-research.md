# Character avatar artwork research

This records earlier research into replacing the original SVG portraits with existing character art. The owner subsequently requested improving the original portraits using supplied images as general visual references instead. No external replacement artwork was included. Character names and likenesses may involve rights beyond the artwork's own license.

## Sources reviewed on 2026-11-12

- The owner's PNGWing [Gandalf](https://www.pngwing.com/en/free-png-amqun) and [Legolas](https://www.pngwing.com/en/free-png-amquy) pages label the images for non-commercial use. [PNGWing's terms](https://www.pngwing.com/terms-of-use) say images are user-submitted and can be from unknown sources; that label alone does not establish the original creator's redistribution permission.
- PNGWing's original downloads require an interactive alphabet challenge. Its accessible 920-pixel previews have a baked-in checkerboard, so they are not suitable transparent assets. Related listings include [Frodo](https://www.pngwing.com/en/free-png-amqvk), [Gollum](https://www.pngwing.com/en/free-png-amquq), and an [elf lady](https://www.pngwing.com/en/free-png-amqvi); the last listing does not definitively identify the character.
- Search results for [Gandalf](https://icons-for-free.com/lord+of+the+rings+old+man+wizard+icon-1320166692548419860/) and [Legolas](https://icons-for-free.com/elf+legolas+lord+of+the+rings+icon-1320166687828491904/) identify Kristian Pettyjohn's *Famous Character Vol 2 Colored* as CC BY 3.0, with a link to Iconfinder. The indexed listing is a promising lead, not confirmation that the PNGWing copies are the same work or that the listing's terms are authoritative. Direct access to the listing and creator's Iconfinder page returned 403 during review. Do not bundle these files until their source and applicable license are verified.
- The owner's [Behance project](https://www.behance.net/gallery/25611567/Lord-of-the-Ring-The-Hobbit-Character-Illustration) is indexed as artwork by Minhye Kim made for a Microsoft project. Behance returned 403 and no reuse license was verified; portfolio visibility alone is not permission to redistribute it.
- [Gandalf by JesicaLR](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gandalf.png) is listed on Wikimedia Commons as CC BY-SA 3.0; Commons separately warns about possible restrictions on fan art. This is a potential licensed individual illustration, not a matching vector set or an approval of the underlying character rights. Confirm attribution, adaptation terms and suitability before use.
- [Icons8](https://icons8.com/icons/set/lord-of-the-rings) offers matching colored Gandalf, Frodo, Legolas and Gollum icons. Its [free attribution page](https://icons8.com/license) allows website use with a link, but its [license agreement](https://intercom.help/icons8-7fb7577e8170/en/articles/5534926-universal-multimedia-licensing-agreement-for-icons8) restricts making assets available as standalone downloadable files; compatibility with this public source repository needs clarification. A matching Sauron was not found.

For a cohesive set, obtain a direct creator/source download with explicit permission for repository distribution and app use, or permission from the relevant artist. Record each file's creator, license, source, modifications and required attribution; do not assume the repository's MIT license covers external artwork.
