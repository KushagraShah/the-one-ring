# Visual assets

`brand/ring.svg` and `brand/mountains.svg` are original artwork created for this project, covered by the repository's MIT license. They use simple geometric shapes and no third-party imagery.

`brand/flying-dragon-openclipart.svg` is [Flying Dragon Silhouette Isolated](https://openclipart.org/detail/315379/flying-dragon-silhouette-isolated), by GDJ via Openclipart, which publishes its uploads under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The source describes it as a remix of a silhouette by matahari22 from Pixabay; see the [upstream remix record](https://openclipart.org/detail/315378/dragon-hovering-over-countryside-silhouette-by-matahari22). The source SVG is retained; the app recolors it red and animates it in CSS. Attribution and license links appear in Settings under Artwork credits. It is not covered by the repository's MIT license.

`avatars/portraits.svg` is an original stylized portrait sprite created for this project, not film artwork or actor likenesses. Its 2026-11-12 revision uses owner-provided illustrations as general references for simple shapes and recognizable silhouettes, not copied image assets. Character names and story elements belong to their respective rights holders; the project license does not grant rights to those underlying properties. Character reference reading included Tolkien Gateway's [Boromir](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Boromir), [Witch-king](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Witch-king), [Galadriel's hair](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hair_of_Galadriel), and [Éowyn](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/%C3%89owyn) pages. Some visual details are artistic interpretations where the texts do not specify a definitive appearance.

Each named SVG in `avatars/` is a standalone, viewable copy of its sprite symbol, including retired characters retained for existing profiles. The app uses the sprite as the single source of truth. After editing it, run `npm run avatars:generate`; `node scripts/avatar-previews.mjs --check` detects out-of-date copies. The generated files include the sprite's shared definitions and are not separate third-party artwork.

### Character detail review

- [Boromir](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Boromir) and [Faramir](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Faramir) describe dark hair and cite Tolkien's later note imagining Aragorn, Denethor and the brothers as beardless. The portraits follow that note; familiar film versions differ. Boromir's horn and Faramir's green hood provide distinguishing context.
- [Treebeard](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Treebeard) is described with a grey beard; [Théoden](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) has white hair. [Shelob](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Shelob) is spider-like with horns; her stylized portrait shows eight legs.
- [Saruman](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Saruman) had mostly white hair with black remaining by the end of the Third Age. His portrait adds dark streaks.
- [Legolas's hair color is disputed](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Legolas/Disputes); the blond portrait is a familiar interpretive choice, not an established book detail. Gimli's beard color, specific armor, the Balrog's silhouette and many hobbit details are also artistic choices, not claims of definitive textual appearance.
- [Lurtz](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Lurtz) originates in the films, not Tolkien's books. The armored Sauron and crowned Witch-king are stylized interpretations; they are not presented as canonical designs or copies of production artwork. No source images are included.
