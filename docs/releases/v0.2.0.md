# v0.2.0 — Workout workflow preview

This is an early preview, not a production-ready or fully offline-safe release.

## What's changed

- A focused phone-first workout flow shows the current exercise, setup notes and recent exact-variant history, with preset reps, a shared-weight control and one log action.
- Sessions support previous/skip/next navigation, temporary replacements and extras, resume/cancel, and automatic finishing once exercises are completed or skipped.
- Routine building has multiple routines, draft/save, exercise ordering, and independent exercise cycles shared across one or more days. A dedicated cycle selector distinguishes cycle variants from replacements and extras.
- The exercise library has optional tags, search/filter and reversible archive. Basic activity and history are available.
- Workout-specific modules have been grouped under `src/workout/`; this is not a complete platform or backend separation.

## Important limitations

- Cloud sync remains experimental and **manual**. Real two-device conflicts, two-user isolation, offline/Android behavior and recovery are not fully verified. Export backups; do not rely on the preview as your only copy.
- Cycle progress is committed when a workout finishes. Existing in-progress sessions retain their prior cycle linkage; older clients do not understand the new cycle semantics. Update all devices and export a backup before relying on changed cycle data.
- Routine drafts and unconfirmed exercise edits are not crash-durable. Browser/phone interaction testing and comprehensive backup recovery remain outstanding.
- Some browser-native prompts remain; day reordering, full planned/performed snapshots and cycle history are incomplete.
